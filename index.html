About The Institute

A Strong Foundation Begins With the Right Guidance.

Narender Thakur's Institute – Shikhar Study Center is an academy in Solan, Himachal Pradesh focused on creating a disciplined and supportive learning environment for students.

Our approach centers on academic guidance, conceptual understanding, and consistent learning — helping students build confidence, stay focused, and move forward with clear direction on their academic journey.

Mentorship

More Than Learning. The Right Direction.

Good learning becomes more effective when students have clarity, structure, and the right direction. Shikhar Study Center focuses on creating an environment where students can stay focused, build consistency, and move toward their academic goals.

Pillar 01

Guidance

Clear direction throughout the learning journey — so students always know where they're going and why.

Pillar 02

Discipline

Pillar 03

Helping students continuously improve their understanding and confidence — one step at a time.
